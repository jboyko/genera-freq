## Project Overview

This is a phylogenetic/macroevolutionary research project investigating the frequency with which large genera evolve, building on Stebbins (1981). The paper draft is at `doc/main.md`.

## Data

- `data/wcvp/wcvp_names.txt` — World Checklist of Vascular Plants (WCVP) taxonomic names
- `data/wcvp/wcvp_distribution.txt` — WCVP species distribution records
- `data/wcvp/wgsrpd-master/` — World Geographic Scheme for Recording Plant Distributions (WGSRPD), a hierarchical 4-level geographic standard used in botanical databases; includes GeoJSON and shapefiles for mapping
- `data/best_wcvp.tre_dated` — dated phylogenetic tree for vascular plants
